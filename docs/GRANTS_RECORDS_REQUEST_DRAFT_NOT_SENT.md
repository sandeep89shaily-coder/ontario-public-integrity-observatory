# Brampton Advance Brampton Fund — focused records enquiry

**Status:** DRAFT ONLY — NOT SENT. No FOI fee authorized.

**To:** abf@brampton.ca
**Subject:** Advance Brampton Fund 2024 public recipient-list revision and 2024–2026 grant reconciliation

We are conducting nonprofit public-interest research on the Advance Brampton Fund. We have reviewed the City's March 7, 2024 announcement referring to 117 funded organizations and the two City-hosted 2024 approved-recipient PDFs, each listing 116 entries. The two PDFs also differ in one $5,000 Arts and Culture entry (and a separate recipient-name formatting change).

Could you please clarify whether a formal list revision, withdrawal, transfer, corrected identification or different counting methodology explains these differences? If already available and disclosable, please identify the initial and revised award approval schedules, dates of revision, any record stating the reason, and a reconciliation of approved 2024 grants and signed agreements. We are not asking for personal bank details.

For a subsequent **program-level** check, could you identify existing 2025–2026 aggregate records showing: applications found eligible/ineligible and approved by stream; three-reviewer evaluation outcomes (redacted or as distributions); total approved versus amounts actually paid; withheld or recovered grant amounts; number of project close-out reports due, accepted and outstanding at the applicable dates; and any published project outcome audits?

We recognize that 2025 grant reporting deadlines differ by stream and that some New and Small closeouts could become due after the September 2025 applications for the 2026 program. We are not asserting that any grant recipient was ineligible or that funds were misused.

Please identify any public or routinely disclosable documents first and advise whether a formal MFIPPA request, redactions or fees are required before processing.
