# Case 002 — Skilled Trades College of Canada: delivery-provider audit

**Research date: 2026-11-20. Scope:** Subcontracted/partner training for Aspire for Higher's SDF-backed Extra-Judicial Employment Fast Track (EJEFT) program. **No findings of fraud, related-party collusion, unregistered training, or missing participants.** No outreach, emails or outside requests without user's explicit approval.

## 1. Verified institutional existence and regulatory context

**Legal identity in educational directory:** 'Skilled Trades College of Canada Inc.' is named in the [Canadian Information Centre for International Credentials (CICIC)](https://www.cicic.ca/873/educational_institution_s_profile.canada?id=9091). It identifies the Toronto East campus at **1448 Lawrence Ave E, Toronto** as a private, for-profit career college, registered/licensed under Ontario's career college framework. A separate CICIC listing covers Vaughan and Oakville. This corroborates real operating education services. It does **not** independently certify that **each** 2024 EJEFT course and **each** campus had active approval at the exact cohort dates: those program/branch approvals require the [Ontario career college search](https://www.ontario.ca/page/search-career-college-ontario).

The college has an independently documented operational history since approximately 2007–08, with a 2021 [Daily Commercial News facility report](https://canada.constructconnect.com/dcn/news/labour/2021/02/enrolment-up-at-skilled-trades-college-due-to-covid-19-pandemic) showing on-site Vaughan teaching. Business directory identifies founder and CEO **Ralph Cerasuolo** (not independently matched to currently registered beneficial owners). **Mike Di Donato** was named 2021 director and February 2026 chief operating officer in a [college-issued Northpine partnership press announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/18/3240276/0/en/Skilled-Trades-College-and-Northpine-Foundation-Joint-Effort-to-Create-Career-Pathways-for-Individuals-Impacted-by-the-Justice-System.html). **No Aspire leadership/board overlap with the college's legal directors has been verified.**

## 2. The College ↔ Aspire program relationship: three documents, three purposes

- [Aspire 2024 impact report](https://aspireforhigher.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2024-A4H-Impact-Report_final.pdf) labels Skilled Trades College of Canada a partner of **EJEFT** and A4H Skills Lab (printed pages 24–25).
- [Aspire Skills Lab's official EJEFT page](https://a4hskillslab.ca/programs/extra-judicial-employment-fast-track-program/) explicitly says a **12-week pre-apprenticeship program led by the Skilled Trades College of Canada**, with Electrical, Plumbing and Intro to Home Renovation options **subsidized by A4H**.
- [City of Vaughan economic development 2024 Steel Toe Careers profile](https://vaughanbusiness.ca/talent-city-grant-recipient-profile-steel-toe-careers/) independently identifies Aspire for Higher among the access-to-training partners of a skilled trades training initiative. This supports an operational collaboration, not a commercial agreement amount. Site may present bot challenge, so indexed excerpt should be independently archived if relied upon publicly.

**Still unavailable:** dated STC–A4H service contract, commercial invoice, bank transfer, course delivery roster, campus assignment, training outcome certification, paid tools invoice, and grant-eligible cost mapping.

## 4. Courses and present-day price benchmark

The [STC admissions page](https://www.skilledtradescollege.ca/admissions), as of Nov 20 2026, states **$14,400** total retail tuition/fees for the 12-week pre-apprenticeship courses. The amount includes high-end tools, textbooks, personal protective equipment and teaching materials; safety boots are separately purchased. A $400 deposit is included in the enrolment process. The fees are a **current publicly advertised individual retail rate**, **NOT a 2024 historical rate**, **NOT a verified A4H procurement price**, and **NOT evidence of actual paid invoices**. Discounted cohort blocks, donor subsidization and different program contracts could have other rates.

STC full-time timetable: **Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–3 PM, for 12 consecutive weeks**. Curriculum is mostly hands-on shop practice. Program graduation is a pre-apprenticeship **training qualification**, not a completed licensed apprenticeship or a guarantee of a construction job. The college expressly says placements cannot be guaranteed. [Official admission information](https://www.skilledtradescollege.ca/admissions).

## 5. Two DIFFERENT 2024 quantitative claims require unit definitions

Aspire's [2024 impact report, printed page 27](https://aspireforhigher.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2024-A4H-Impact-Report_final.pdf) includes a category:
- **'Skilled Trades College of Canada Scholarship Instruction'**: **65 adults served**, **31,200 hours attained**.
- Mathematically **31,200 / 65 = 480 counted hours per adult**, exactly.
- The STC current advertised *clock* timetable of 8:30–15:00 for five weekdays across 12 weeks yields **6.5 × 5 × 12 = 390 gross scheduled hours**, even before breaks (90 below 480).
- This does **not** show that hours were fabricated: 480 may reflect prescribed/academic credit hours, a broader package including other activities, cohort-specific schedules or historical program definitions. Conversely it may be an estimate or mislabeling. **Obtain an explicit hours-definition and original college certified student-hours before drawing a conclusion.**
- The same 2024 impact report, printed page 25, **separately** claims **52 EJEFT graduates** and **34 jobs created**. Do **not** assume those 52 form the subset of the 65 scholarship participants until cohort overlap is established, even though 52/65 would be 80%.

Source observation note: the 38 MB 2024 PDF yields indexed text but the web screenshot service rejected it owing to size. Claims have been read from the text layer and require independent page-image archival review for high-stakes publication.

## 7. Additional college partner — Northpine Foundation

A [February 18, 2026 college press release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/18/3240276/0/en/Skilled-Trades-College-and-Northpine-Foundation-Joint-Effort-to-Create-Career-Pathways-for-Individuals-Impacted-by-the-Justice-System.html) announces a **separate one-year, potentially fully funded tuition initiative** for justice-involved individuals with the Northpine Foundation. It is **not evidence that the Northpine program subsidized A4H trainees**, nor that the same participants were double-funded. Program similarities make it worthwhile to reconcile scholarship and tuition-source fields *if* original anonymized cohort records are obtained. No links between Northpine and any specific A4H grant payments are proven.

## 8. Records that would conclusively answer value-for-money and delivery questions

1. For each A4H SDF cohort: campus address, start/end date, STC program approval/registration status, curriculum, capacity, actual enrolment, withdrawals and completions (anonymized cohort totals).
2. **College × Aspire contract**, quote/competitive procurement where applicable, historical per-seat/flat rate, detailed invoice and its settlement date, and any volume discount, tuition scholarships or separate public grants paid to the college.
3. Itemized **tool kits / PPE** procured/delivered, serial or custody logs where used, wholesaler receipts, and whether tools are included in tuition.
4. **Contact hours**: definition of 31,200 'hours attained', 65 adults, per-cohort instructional timetable, independent academic-hour vs clock-hour reconciliation.
5. **Employment**: acceptable definitions for 34 'jobs created' (actual hired people, 60-day retained employment, internships or job opportunities), independently employer-certified anonymized outcomes, and whether they are the same as 52 graduates or 31 placements/14,880 hours separately listed.
6. **Ministry SDF TPA**: approved project cost/trainee targets, selection rationale, amounts advanced, reimbursement claims, holdbacks and accepted outcomes. A general college tuition figure cannot account for the full reported $4.5m SDF award, which may include wages, wraparound supports and other legitimate items.

## Source status and communications

The Ministry's [career college compliance/enforcement dataset](https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/private-career-colleges-pcc-notices-orders-and-financial-penalties) exists, but its current CSV returned HTTP 429 and was **not successfully checked** for this institution; **do not assert either a clean compliance record or an enforcement finding**.

No direct colleges, participants, employers, administrators or grant recipients were contacted, and no email/FOI filings were sent.

**Assessment:** STC is a publicly recognized functioning career college with a documented A4H training relationship. The case's strongest unresolved checks are the **historic commercial tuition contract, locations actually attended by funded cohorts, definition of 31,200 hours, certified completions and employer-verified job placements**. None of the above supplies evidence of fraud.
