### Fixed questions for a meaningful investigation
1. Verify corporation ID, board at each grant decision date and whether legal recipient behind 2024 variant is identical to federal BN and later City years.
2. Find 2024–26 **exact grant-funded project titles, delivery locations, approved expenses, three review scores, approved matching contributions and decision dates**. A switch between Arts and Culture and Recreation may be normal because projects differ.
3. Compare **event dates and venue bookings** to funded project schedules, and obtain anonymized attendance/outcome totals and accepted City close-outs.
4. Identify how 2025 ESDC wage-subsidy staff hours and duties relate to the 2025 City-funded project. Wage support and City grant can legitimately support different expenses; duplicate claims require invoices or payroll records before any assertion.
5. Do the small councillor-office sponsorship entries have any relation to the **specific ABF evaluation/approval decision**, and were they properly reported? No influence or conflict has been established.
6. Use the agency's website, Board of Trade profile and third-party technology donation to corroborate actual operations; absence of extensive online documentation is not a misconduct indicator.

**Preliminary assessment:** source-backed, low-profile lead; verified awards and program activities, but no verified wrongdoing. No external communication is authorized.
