# Brampton Advance Brampton Fund — process and receipts audit

**Investigation priority:** actual eligibility, fairness of selection, approval authority, genuine expenses/payments, matching contributions, and whether promised activities occurred. *Award-value rankings and name repetition are not misconduct evidence.* No messages may be sent without specific user approval.

## Applicable official policy (2025 pilot)

[2025 Fund Guidelines](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund%20Guidelines%20V1.0.pdf), especially sections 6.3 (eligible organization evidence), 8–10 (review, approvals and payment), 11–12 (close-out and controls).

1. **Applicant legitimacy:** Registered charities required CRA evidence; incorporated nonprofits required corporate profile; eligible unincorporated nonprofits required two authorized members, board list and governance document. Some otherwise legitimate grantees will not appear on CRA's charity list. Verify the correct category, historical registration, Brampton address, eligibility application and organizational signatories. Use only lawful, public sources; never publish private address, photo ID or bank information. [Ontario Business Registry](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-business-registry); [CRA registered charity resources](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/charities/information-about-a-charity.html).
2. **Selection:** Confirm application completeness and submission timestamp; obtain three reviewer score sheets, the declared criteria and actual average (published minimum 50% New/Small, 60% Developing, 70% Amplifying), score chronology, documented conflicts or recusals, staff recommendation, Commissioner approval. Passing score does not guarantee award. Verify exceptions/changes before any inference.
3. **Payment:** Obtain executed grant agreement, City payment ledger and dates, payment authorization, amendments, holdbacks, recoveries and reconciled project amounts. Published approval is NOT a payment. For 2025, New/Small 100% was scheduled after agreement; Developing/Amplifying 90% plus 10% after an accepted close-out.
4. **Genuine expenses:** Obtain source invoice, vendor and item/quantity/date, matching worksheet, original supporting records, separately documented proof of payment, and independent supplier confirmation as appropriate. Compare invoices against City-approved categories, dates, duplicate expense allocations, vendor identity and permitted matching rules, including volunteer and in-kind contributions. An invoice PDF can be forged and a bank debit can be unrelated. For taxable invoices claiming GST/HST, [CRA's GST/HST registry](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/e-services/digital-services-businesses/confirming-a-gst-hst-account-number.html) can help verify number, business name and transaction date. Not all legitimate suppliers are registered or should charge GST/HST. **Never declare a receipt fraudulent by appearance or mere number overlap.**
5. **Real delivery:** Obtain application-specific deliverables, amended scope approvals, project end date, original close-out report, expense worksheet, City acceptance, and independently verifiable output evidence: dated attendance counts, venue records, third-party partner confirmation or beneficiary surveys where consent permits. City close-out acceptance alone is not independently verified delivery.

## Explicitly missing from publicly posted recipient PDFs

- Individual applications, proof of eligibility at submission and complete reviewer score sheets.
- Conflict-of-interest declarations and reviewer-to-applicant relationship tests.
- Commissioner-signed decision records, grant agreements, actual City transfer dates.
- Original expense receipts/invoices, payment proofs, matching contributions and independent supplier confirmations.
- Accepted close-out/impact reports and independently verified participation/delivery results.

**Important:** These are **not publicly observed**; that does not establish the City lacks them, the recipients failed to submit them, or any wrongdoing occurred. A formal disclosure route may require redactions or lawful exemptions. Sensitive documents belong in private restricted storage, not public GitHub Actions artifacts.

## Reproducible first sample

The automated audit selects the first two published 2025 awards alphabetically in each grant stream: **six neutral demonstration cases**, not a ranking or suspicion shortlist. For each, it produces seven stage checks (42 total). With only the published grant lists, all 42 are **NOT_OBTAINED** because no application, score, payment or receipt exhibits were supplied. This is a transparent evidence-access gap, not a finding against any organization.

## Next substantive evidence acquisition (requires explicit approval before any message)

The most decisive initial records are (1) redacted original application/evaluation/approval packet for a small cross-stream sample, (2) City-issued payment transactions and grant agreement amounts, and (3) 2025 paid expense closeout packages and approved outcome reports, in redacted form and with clear source provenance. We can draft but must **not send** records requests, pay FOI fees, contact grantees or communicate with vendors without the user's approval of each action.

Review every item with date-specific applicable policies; an unincorporated group can be legitimate and 2026 application timing precedes some 2025 closeout deadlines. Never score an identifiable recipient as "fraud risk" without corroborated documentary evidence.
