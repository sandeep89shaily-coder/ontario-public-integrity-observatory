## Why this is a serious documentary candidate, rather than selecting an unfamiliar name

A lesser-known seniors nonprofit is named in three consecutive City of Brampton Advance Brampton Fund approved lists, plus a federal NHSP agreement. **Two different federal corporation numbers** and a clear historic incorporation-dissolution-reincorporation timeline for the same name create an objective entity-continuity question.

## Two corporate identities — secondary federal data needs primary-source confirmation

- Original **Southlake Seniors Club Canada Foundation**, federal corporation **14532546**, business number **790296214**, incorporated **November 15, 2022**, dissolved **June 16, 2023**. Historical directors as indexed: Gian Paul, Amit Bhatt, Ram Daind. [Indexed federal corporation record](https://federalcorporation.ca/corporation/14532546).
- Second federal corporation **15154596**, business number **740454616**, incorporated **June 27, 2023**, *active* in published corporate data. Directors indexed: Amit Bhatt, Vishnukumar S Patel, Rehana S Hafeez. [Second corporate record](https://federalcorporation.ca/corporation/15154596).
- The original was dissolved **11 days before** the successor was incorporated, both with an indexed director named Amit Bhatt. It is **not yet verified** that one legally succeeded the other by an assignment or asset transfer, or that such transfer was required for City awards. A dissolved company cannot itself enter a later new agreement, but the later corporation appears active and can do so in its own right. No evidence the old dissolved entity applied for a 2024–26 grant.
- Independently, the [2026 federal NHSP grant disclosure](https://search.open.canada.ca/grants/record/esdc-edsc%2C014-2025-2026-Q4-01379%2Ccurrent) identifies recipient business number **740454616** — **the 2023 second corporation**, resolving **that specific federal award**. It does NOT identify the contracting BN of any individual Brampton ABF grant.
- Verify original records using [Corporations Canada search](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cc/lgcy/fdrlCrpSrch.html); secondary aggregators are not sufficient for a published adverse finding. No private director residences are required.

## Activity corroboration (independent municipal publication)

The [City of Brampton Adult 55+ Community Groups directory](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/Residents/Recreation/Connections/Pages/Community-Groups-Adults55.aspx) explicitly describes Southlake as providing seniors' skills, activities and talent development, and lists meetings **on second Saturday of every month and special occasions** at **Century Gardens Recreation Centre** and **Jim Archdekin Recreation Centre**, contact **Amit Bhatt**. This supports a publicly identified operating club and meeting venues, not a 2024–26 project-specific grant closeout or audited attendance.

A [City spring 2024 council newsletter](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/Council-Office/Newsletters/Documents/Wards%201%265%20Spring%202024%20Council%20Newsletter%20-%20Digital.pdf) depicts or discusses Southlake community involvement; publication alone is not assurance that grant-funded activities occurred.

## Documented network — separate affiliations versus same recipient

1. Corporate indexes list **Amit Bhatt** as director of both historical and successor Southlake corporations, **Maa Laxmi Enterprise Inc.** (same commercially formatted suite address as second Southlake corporation), and the **Federation of Canada India Commerce & Industry**. These are records to validate by dated original filings; they do **not** establish that those companies supplied goods to Southlake or received its grant money.
2. The first Southlake corporation's indexed director **Gian Paul** is also listed in **Mukti Foundation** and **PSB Seniors Club Association** records. The older group's former director **Ram Daind** also appears in the latter's corporate directory. Brampton's 2024 grants list **PSB Seniors Club Association** for a separate $5,000 award. This is a **historical governance-overlap lead**, not proof the second Southlake corporation was commonly controlled with PSB or that grants were pooled.
3. A **2026 mayoral campaign media release** publicly lists Amit Bhatt in his capacity as **President, Southlake Seniors Club Canada Foundation**, among endorsers. [Campaign's own release](https://votepatrickbrown.com/media/gujarati-community-endorsement/). This is evidence of a public political endorsement, **not** evidence of an improper municipal funding decision, a financial interest in a councillor, or unlawful election conduct. Political beliefs and endorsements are not used to score or label grant recipients.
4. A public federal grant with BN 740454616 gives one precise legal identity anchor — focus on matching each municipal 2024–26 signed agreement to that BN rather than inferring from the name.
