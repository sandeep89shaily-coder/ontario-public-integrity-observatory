# Ontario Public Integrity Observatory — investigative remit, November 2026

## Decision

The first priority is **decision and money trails**, not ranking vendors by frequency of contract wins.

The investigated questions fall into five evidence-based workstreams:

1. **Procurement exceptions and manipulation hypotheses** — competitive vs non-competitive, exception authority, missing bids, split acquisitions, procurement decisions and rationale.
2. **Contract amendments and spending** — original vs revised scope/approved amount, change orders, actual payments and deliverables, on identical accounting bases.
3. **Grants and transfers** — recipients, awards, eligibility, scoring/rationale, reporting and deliverables, repeat funding, related-party relationships where independently established.
4. **Declared interests and decision participation** — public disclosures, council minutes, recusals, decision chronology, independently verified corporate identities. A missing web hit is not evidence of failure to disclose.
5. **Municipal decisions and accountability** — competing explanations, council/staff approvals, auditor or integrity commissioner findings, responses, corrections.

## First official sources

- [Mississauga purchasing reports](https://www.mississauga.ca/services-and-programs/business/doing-business-with-the-city/purchasing-reports/). Posted thresholds: $125,000 noncompetitive and $325,000 competitive. Missing small purchases are outside this publication coverage.
- [Toronto April 8, 2026 General Government Committee decisions](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/report.do?meeting=2026.GG28&type=decisions). Includes documented purchase-order amendments and decisions; increases by themselves do not imply impropriety.
- [Brampton 2026 Advance Brampton Fund recipients](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2026%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2026-Advance-Brampton-Fund-Approved-Recipients.pdf) and [2025 recipients](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund%20Approved%20Applicants%20%28For%20Web%20V.%201%29.pdf).
- [Brampton declarations register entry point](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/Council-Committees/Pages/Term-of-Council-Declarations-of-Interest.aspx) and [Toronto declared-interest registry](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/accountability-operations-customer-service/access-city-information-or-records/declared-interests-registry/).

## Important safeguards

- Acquisition exceptions may be lawfully authorized; repetitions or round values are not standalone evidence of improper conduct.
- Public grants are not questionable simply because a recipient is repeated or receives a large amount.
- Contract increases can follow legitimate scope, schedule or tax adjustments; verify the City's recorded explanation.
- A declaration registry may be incomplete, unavailable, or not relevant to an administrative procurement. Do not label anyone undisclosed without direct corroboration.
- No automated actor risk score, no public allegation, no finding of fraud without thorough human-reviewed corroboration.
- This pipeline creates source-linked leads and questions, not finished journalism.
- No use of private GTA Radar data, secrets or paid lead scoring.
