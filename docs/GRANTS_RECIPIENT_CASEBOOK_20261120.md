# Brampton Advance Brampton Fund: recipient continuity, purposes and documentary relationships

**First issue: 2026-11-20.** Focus: **2024–2026 only**, 412 City-listed approved entries. This is documentary research, not a determination of fraud or misconduct.

[Live GitHub Actions run](https://github.com/sandeep89shaily-coder/ontario-public-integrity-observatory/actions/runs/44833926098) generated a source-linked full casebook of **109 repeated normalized recipient-name groups**, **35 appearing across all three years**, **15 changing priority area**, and **57 changing grant stream**. Identity and legal relationships are **not automatically verified**. The generated award-level source records link back to each annual City PDF and page. No proven improper relationships or payments have been identified.

## Four illustrative research leads (NOT suspected misconduct)

### 1. The FOLD Foundation — repeating awards and multiple publicly listed public-funding programs

- 2024 Advance Brampton Fund: **$25,000**, Arts and Culture / Amplifying.
- 2025: **$23,276**, Arts and Culture / Amplifying.
- 2026: **$21,250**, Arts and Culture / Amplifying.
- **Approved three-year total: $69,526**. These are approved City ABF amounts, not City payment confirmations.
- The Foundation's [March 2024 announcement](https://thefoldcanada.org/the-festival-of-literary-diversity-2024/) describes the April–May 2024 literary festival and identifies Brampton among the supporters. The City also lists [The FOLD under sponsored festivals](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/Arts-Culture-Tourism/Festivals-and-Events/Pages/City-Sponsored-Festivals.aspx). This confirms publicly described activity and a City-supported cultural program, **not that a particular ABF award financed any specific festival costs**.
- Independent program context: [federal grants and contributions record](https://search.open.canada.ca/grants/record/pch%2C016-2020-2021-Q2-1332608%2Ccurrent) lists a FOLD literary activity. Do not confuse federal funding, ABF funding, or City festival sponsorship.
- **Questions:** Which project exactly was covered by each year's ABF agreement? If the same event had multiple funding sources, what were each instrument's permitted costs and were the same invoices charged twice? No duplication is established by the records reviewed.

### 2. BMG-Brilliant Minds Association — multi-year award and legal identity pathway

- 2024: **$24,800**, Community Safety / Amplifying.
- 2025: **$22,416**, Community Safety / Amplifying.
- 2026: **$21,640.50**, Community Safety / Amplifying.
- **Approved three-year total: $68,856.50**.
- An [official Corporations Canada record](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cc/lgcy/fdrlCrpDtls.html?corpId=12660300&lang=eng) provides an organizational registration route. [Brilliant Minds Group's public website](https://www.bmggrp.ca/about/) describes mentorship, career and newcomer-support activities. Neither establishes which specific grant-funded projects ran in 2024–2026 or what their receipts showed.
- **Questions:** Verify that legal entity was grant applicant for each year; obtain the actual grant project title, agreement and closeout; verify public outcome evidence. No relationship to another ABF recipient has been established.

### 3. Vision of Hope Resource Centre — changing public priority-area label

- The City lists **Recreation** in 2024 and **Community Safety** in 2025 and 2026 under the same published name. This is a **classification change**, not evidence of a false application or a linked organization.
- **Questions:** Were the proposed activities actually different? Were any application criteria or stream requirements changed? Were prior project deliverables reviewed when the subsequent applications were assessed?

### 4. SAATH Foundation — change in grant stream

- City-listed awards: **$25,000 Amplifying (2024)**, **$20,000 Amplifying (2025)**, **$12,500 Developing (2026)**; **$57,500** total.
- **Questions:** Why was the 2026 application assessed in a different stream? A project's scope, budget and submission choice may legitimately differ across years; don't infer sanction, preference or misconduct.

## Actual relationships — what is documented?

**Zero cross-recipient ownership, directorship or payment relationships verified in this pilot.** Joint appearances on a recipient list or in the same priority area do not create a connection. The system will only accept a dated, reviewed primary-source statement of registered identity, officer overlap, disclosed partnership or financial transfer. A shared name alone is insufficient to identify an individual or director.

Possible official sources: [CRA charity records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/charities/information-about-a-charity.html), [Ontario Business Registry](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-business-registry), and Federal Corporations Canada, plus public audited accounts and explicit primary-source partnership reports. Avoid exposing personal residential addresses, banking information or unsupported family ties.

## Critical missing information

- **Specific funded purpose:** the public approved-recipient PDFs contain a broad priority area but generally not the actual proposed project title/description. [2025 guidelines](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund%20Guidelines%20V1.0.pdf), section 13, indicate that City-funded activity names and descriptions are public information. Locate the project-description schedules before comparing supposed project duplication.
- **Selection:** eligibility checks, reviewer scores, amendments, decision dates, conflict disclosures.
- **Spending:** approved vs actual City payments, original invoices, proof of purchase, matching funds and document authenticity.
- **Delivery:** on-the-ground events and independently corroborated outputs, closeout reports and acceptance.
- **Relationships:** exact legal identifiers and dated evidence between named recipient entities and any suppliers or grant decision-makers.
