**This is a publishing-rule reconciliation question, not an irregular procurement finding.** An official explanation could involve cumulative purchase value, revised approvals, classification rules, older thresholds, or inclusion for public transparency. Do not assume the indexed threshold applies per printed vendor line. Verify with City procurement staff.

## Contract-amendment review example

[Toronto General Government Committee decision GG28.2](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/report.do?meeting=2026.GG28&type=decisions) records the WSP Canada Inc. purchase order 6051950 revision from $3,180,416 to $4,944,893 net of applicable taxes, with an increase of $1,764,477. The Council item explains that the City changed the delivery approach, splitting construction work into four contracts and modifying administration and inspection requirements. This is a **published explanation**, not evidence of wrongdoing. Investigate the original scope, approvals, change orders, final costs, and whether any subsequent contracting followed documented authority.
