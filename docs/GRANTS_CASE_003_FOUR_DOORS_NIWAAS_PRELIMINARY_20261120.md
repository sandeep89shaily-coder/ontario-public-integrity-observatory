This is a **different Brampton grant program** from Advance Brampton Fund (ABF): the Community Improvement Plan for Affordable Housing funded from federal Housing Accelerator Fund and Ontario Building Faster Fund. ## Council governance and reported allegations

A [December 18, 2025 investigation by The Pointer](https://thepointer.com/article/2025-12-18/brampton-deputy-mayor-harkirat-singh-helped-direct-24m-in-taxpayer-funding-to-his-own-corporation) reported that Deputy Mayor **Harkirat Singh** supported and voted on grant-related items and did not declare a conflict at the relevant meetings, despite earlier links to Niwaas Long-Term Care and launch of Niwaas Living. The article identified unresolved due diligence and land-control questions. **This is a reporter's account, not a court finding.** Singh said he had **no direct or indirect pecuniary interest**, **received no compensation**, and **consulted the Integrity Commissioner**. He said the two Niwaas organizations are separate and that the LTC fundraising cabinet does not fundraise for Living.

A *potential nonpecuniary organizational association* must not be presented as an established legal violation of Ontario's Municipal Conflict of Interest Act. The proper examination is original declarations, minutes, voting record, dated director or founder relationship, legal conflict standard, and any public written integrity opinion if disclosed.

An [official City biography](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/Council-Office/Harkirat-Singh) describes Niwaas Long-Term Care's launching of Niwaas Living, which is a publicly acknowledged connection; the degree and dates of legal control remain to be verified from dated filings.
