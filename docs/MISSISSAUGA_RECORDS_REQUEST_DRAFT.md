# Draft information request — not sent

**To:** City of Mississauga Procurement Services, via the City's official routine-disclosure/request process.

**Subject:** Request for contract-performance and completion records — six identified procurement files

We are conducting an independent, nonprofit public-interest study on municipal procurement and project delivery. Please advise what records can be supplied through routine disclosure, and whether a formal MFIPPA request would be necessary for any remaining records.

The relevant City procurement identifiers are PRC005202, PRC005487, PRC005464, PRC005502, PRC005342, and PRC005414.

For each file, where already existing and disclosable, we request the executed contract or summary of scope, original and revised schedule, award decision date, record of approved amendments/change orders, final payments or final contract cost (if completed), substantial-completion/acceptance dates, formal deficiency or corrective-action notices, and supplier performance evaluations. For ongoing projects, please indicate whether interim evaluations exist; no evaluation should be created in response to this request.

To assess the effect of past performance on subsequent awards, please also identify any already existing record documenting whether earlier performance was reviewed during later procurement decisions, subject to lawful exemptions.

Electronic records and existing indexes are preferred. Please flag any foreseeable processing costs or confidentiality exemptions before proceeding.

This research does not allege wrongdoing by the City or any supplier.

**Status:** Draft only. No message has been sent, no FOI fee paid, and no information request has been submitted.

Official process: https://www.mississauga.ca/our-organization/submit-a-freedom-of-information-request/
