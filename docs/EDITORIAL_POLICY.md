# Editorial and investigation policy

1. Investigate public-interest questions, never assign automatic fraud guilt or risk scores to identifiable individuals.
2. Preserve exact sources, document hashes, dates, quoted passages, and uncertainty.
3. Verify corporate identity before linking records under one contractor.
4. Investigate explanations including market concentration, compliant procurement exceptions, scope changes and municipal-caused delays.
5. Give affected organizations reasonable opportunity to respond before publishing adverse claims.
6. Protect personal information, withhold unreviewed allegations and record corrections visibly.
7. Treat AI models as assistants, not sources of independently verified facts.
8. Follow source licences, access restrictions and publisher terms.
