# Grant investigation case 001: Brampton's 2024 recipient-list revisions

**Status:** Documentary reconciliation pending. **No allegations or findings of wrongdoing.** November 20, 2026.

## Question

Why does Brampton's March 7, 2024 funding announcement refer to 117 organizations, when **both** City-hosted versions of its 2024 public grant recipients PDF contain 116 award-list entries? What explains a recipient name change between those versions, and how does this relate, if at all, to executed agreements and payments?

## Primary records, independently fetched by GitHub Actions

1. [March 7, 2024 City announcement](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/News/Pages/News-Release.aspx/1347): **117 non-profit organizations and charities**, nearly $1.5 million.
2. [Earlier City-hosted 2024 recipient PDF](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2024%20Approved%20ABF%20Applicants%20%28For%20Web%29.pdf): 116 extractable approved entries; City-hosted source SHA256 `090cd6eea958e9135f5ed360724f699e70c777e54244a114f1a8943d2c6a8a8c`.
3. [Currently linked revised 2024 recipient PDF (V.2)](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2024%20Approved%20ABF%20Applicants%20%28For%20Web%29%20V.2.pdf): 116 extractable approved entries. See SHA in the run artifact.
4. [Successful GitHub Actions source comparison](https://github.com/sandeep89shaily-coder/ontario-public-integrity-observatory/actions/runs/44825481316): saved `archived_2024_version_comparison.json`; PDF parses reported **zero held rows** for the original.

## Exact documented version differences

- One `New and Small` / `Arts and Culture` **$5,000** entry changes from **TRISTAN CARLOS CLARKE** (earlier list) to **The Spades Family Foundation** (revised V.2). This is a revision of *listed recipient names*. It is NOT confirmation of a payment, cancellation, substitution of legal grantee, or a compliance violation.
- Another $5,000 entry appears with typography changed from **Nandi Films & Entertainment** to **Nandi Films and Entertainment**. This is a plausible presentation or name-standardization change; not evidence of a distinct recipient.
- Both versions have 116 entries and the same sum of published approved awards, **$1,474,770**. The announcement count of 117 and its description of *organizations* are not necessarily directly comparable to an award-list entry count.

## Distinguish questions from conclusions

- The two PDFs are source evidence for different published lists; neither proves which legal entity signed the agreement or received funds.
- The revised filename does not establish a revision date or reason.
- The press release may count organizations differently, or the record may have been corrected after publication.
- 2024 rules also contemplate unincorporated groups. Do not assume individual-name publication was an eligibility breach.

## What could close the question

Ask the City for **existing** records: original 2024 approval roster and delegated approval date, updated roster and change-approval/reason if recorded, a reconciliation of 117 vs 116, and confirmation whether the relevant $5,000 record was amended, withdrawn, renamed or changed in the public list. Seek an aggregate, redacted approval-to-payment schedule rather than private financial records.

If the City supplies an administrative explanation corroborated by documents, close this case accordingly. No public adverse inference should be made without the correct facts, date chronology and right of reply.

## Next grants-only work (fixed scope)

After reconciling 2024 versions, request 2025–2026 **aggregate** approval-to-actual-payment and closeout-by-stream reconciliations. Cross-year name repetition is an input to a continuity check, not an investigative allegation.
