# Focused investigation: Brampton Advance Brampton Fund

**2026-11-20 decision:** Investigate municipal grants first. Procurement, contractor history, and council change-order work remains preserved but inactive. The active grant research cycle is **2024–2026**; do not expand cities or grant programs until one complete end-to-end investigation is tested.

## The evidence chain

1. Who was **approved**, by stream and year? Read original [2024](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2024%20Approved%20ABF%20Applicants%20%28For%20Web%29%20V.2.pdf), [2025](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund%20Approved%20Applicants%20%28For%20Web%20V.%201%29.pdf), and [2026](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2026%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2026-Advance-Brampton-Fund-Approved-Recipients.pdf) recipient PDFs. Preserve source page, SHA and exact amount. Name matches are not legal entity matches.
2. What evaluation and **approval controls** applied? The [2025 guidelines](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund%20Guidelines%20V1.0.pdf) specify administrative eligibility review, three evaluators, minimum scores of 50%/60%/70% depending on stream, and delegated final approval. Actual score sheets and decisions are not in the public recipient list.
3. What was **paid**? In 2025 New and Small projects were payable 100% after signed agreements; Developing and Amplifying projects were payable 90% initially and 10% upon accepted closeout. These are policy schedules, **not verified payment events**.
4. What was **delivered and checked**? Retrieve project end dates, submitted closeout reports, City acceptance, outcomes, withheld funds, cancelled/recovered awards and lawful exceptions. 2025 New and Small reports could be due March 1, 2026, after the 2026 intake application deadline; use the legally appropriate decision date.
5. **Compare like with like**. Brampton's [arts dashboard](https://performancedashboard.brampton.ca/culture-community-well-being/grant-funding-directed-to-arts-and-culture-programs/) reports a February 2026 **2025 estimate** of $646,244 and 51 projects, with a disbursement-based definition. Do not call a difference from approved awards missing money without matching populations and accounting dates.

## Output

Grant-only source ledger, totals by grant stream, neutral cross-year exact name matches, hold/error file, and a draft *unsent* routine disclosure enquiry focused on aggregate payments, closeouts and evaluator scoring. No corruption labels, no unverified human relationship graphs, no automatic adverse publication.

**Next acceptance:** Check totals against City's published releases; verify stream classification by source page; obtain payment/closeout/approval records. Only thereafter draft a human-reviewed, attributable investigation.
