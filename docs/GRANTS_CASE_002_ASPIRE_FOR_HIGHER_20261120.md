# Case 002 — Aspire for Higher: cross-government grants and organization relationships

**As of November 20, 2026. Status: source-backed public accountability investigation, NOT a fraud allegation. No direct outreach authorized.**

## Why this case

The next Brampton ABF recipient investigation is **Aspire for Higher**, because it combines three differently styled municipal award names with substantial, documented federal and provincial funding and one third-party accounting connection. We are selecting it because original agreements, program budgets and payment records could answer precise questions. We do **not** characterize the organization or its directors as corrupt or fraudulent.

## 3. What organization says it delivered

The [organization's 2025 impact report](https://aspireforhigher.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-A4H-Impact-Report.pdf) self-reports 7,734 youths and 4,930 adults served, 130 jobs created; **A4H Skills Lab** claims 28 pre-apprenticeship graduates, 23 placements, 5,000+ placement hours. These figures are **not independently checked in this case**. They are nevertheless specific claims that can be reconciled to funding conditions, eligibility, independent training credentials, employers, project expenses and formal government acceptance.

The City's [independent youth-sports listing](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/residents/Recreation/Connections/pages/youth-sports.aspx) publicly recognizes Aspire for Higher as offering youth sports and mental-wellness programming. This supports an actual local program presence but is **not a certification of any ABF grant project completion**.

Only compare **dated** board terms and statutory filings before asking a governance question.

## 5. Substantive third-party financial connection

[Skills For Life Inc's independently audited accounts for FY ended August 31, 2025](https://theskills4life.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2024-25-Audited-Financial-Statement-HRP-1-compressed.pdf), **note 5**, state that **$12,500 was due to 'Aspire 4 Higher' as funds held in trust**. This is genuine documentary evidence of a liability owed to an Aspire-named entity, **not proof that the $12,500 arose from the City of Brampton's identical-amount 2024 award**.

Items still unverified: beneficiary legal business number, trust agreement and purpose, source of the money, period and subsequent settlement, applicable permitted uses, and whether either named party received or reported a related Brampton grant. The financial statement's note also describes trust funds as liabilities and separately recognizes administration fees. **No improper conduct is established.**

## 6. Fixed investigative tests

1. **Identity**: exact registered number and contracting legal person on each municipal ABF agreement.
2. **Selection**: grant application stream, budget, exact project title, three scoring sheets, eligibility decision and approver.
3. **Actual spending**: City payment ledger plus original vendor invoices, matched supplier documentation, matching-fund evidence and close-out acceptance; do not infer from announced amounts.
4. **Ontario transfer outcome**: what MLITSD programs and tranches make up the $1,773,039 in FY2024–25, which skills cohorts were funded and which employer, participant and credential outcomes were independently verified.
5. **Successive BHPN awards**: distinguish 2021–24 BHPN deliverables from 2026–29 planned healthcare incubator, milestones, advance payments and per-project cost allocation.
6. **Trust relationship**: legal identity and actual funding provenance for the $12,500 owed by Skills For Life to Aspire 4 Higher. The matching number is a **reconciliation cue only**.
7. **Right of reply and legitimate explanations**: no adverse publication before original records, independent corroboration and fair opportunity to respond.

**Research limitation:** no original Brampton grant agreements, receipts, individual participant payment records, trust contract, invoices or complete agency performance files were obtained here. They are needed for any substantive misuse-of-funds claim. No emails, FOI forms or external requests have been sent.
