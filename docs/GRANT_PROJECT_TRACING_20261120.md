# Focused 2024–2026 grant-project tracing — first public evidence checks

**Purpose:** identify the exact project *claimed* by a repeat grant recipient, see other publicly reported funders, and investigate what can be corroborated. **No conclusion of fraud or duplicate claiming.** The City has NOT supplied original applications, approved expense budgets, receipts, project close-out or payment schedules. No email was sent.

## BMG–Brilliant Minds Association

The City's annual approved lists report $24,800 (2024), $22,416 (2025) and $21,640.50 (2026), totalling **$68,856.50** in approved city grants. The same printed recipient name is present in each year, but corporate legal identity across awards is not independently reconciled.

Separate project-level, **self-reported** activity evidence:

- **2024:** [BMG's public webinar promotion](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bmg-brilliant-minds-group_bmg-in-partnership-with-abf-advance-brampton-activity-7247221410425647104-GMuA) describes an ABF-linked three-month mentoring program with 11 weekly webinars starting September 18, 2024, and a collaboration with A1 Global College. This is planned/promoted activity evidence.
- **2025:** [BMG's own mentorship program page](https://www.bmggrp.ca/mentorship/) describes an ABF mentorship program with **four planned cohorts**, six weekly career workshops per cohort and two-month mentorship periods.
- **2025 reported delivery:** A [public coordinator's account of the June 20 graduation and job fair](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bhandarimani_graduationceremony-jobfair-activity-7343294104044191744-CQhF) says two cohorts were delivered and names A1 Global College as a collaborator. These are a program organizer's reported claims, not an independently audited completion report, attendee totals or invoices.
- **2026:** [BMG's own post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bmg-brilliant-minds-group_bmg-abf-mentorshipprogram-activity-7447642446097829888-Cobl) announced a 2026 ABF mentorship workshop for April 22. That is planned/promoted activity evidence, NOT independently verified attendance or City project acceptance.

**Specific next checks:** Did the City-approved project proposals for all three years actually fund these advertised cohorts? What were promised cohort size, eligibility, output measures and expense lines? Which workshops were delivered, with independent attendee or venue confirmation? Was money transferred to or invoiced by collaborators? A public collaboration does **not** establish a payment relationship. Is there an original receipt plus proof of payment and legitimate supplier invoice for each claimed expense? What did the City accept in each close-out?

## Skills For Life Inc — other public funder, distinct funding program

The City's Brampton ABF published lists show **$25,000 (2024), $17,500 (2025) and $20,000 (2026)** under the same printed name: **$62,500 of listed ABF awards**.

Separately, the **Region of Peel**, in its [Community Investment Program official public listing](https://peelregion.ca/business/community-partnerships/community-investment-program/community-investment-program-funded-programs), reports:

- **2024 Core Fund: $69,200**, for general life skills development.
- **2025 Core Fund: $5,587**, again described as life skills development.

This is a **multi-funder recipient continuity question**, not proof of duplicative funding: Peel *Core Fund* and City of Brampton *Advance Brampton Fund* are different programs, and their costs, dates and allowable budget lines may differ. The two Peel values are **not added to the City's $62,500** in the casebook as if they were a single source or verified actual receipts.

**Specific next checks:** Verify legal entity continuity, read Brampton's actual project titles and budgets for each year, check which Peel costs are core operations vs discrete deliverables, and compare invoices/approved matching-fund sources only if those records become available. Without that, financial overlap cannot be established.

## Evidence hierarchy and decision rules

- City-approved grant line = confirmed **reported award** only.
- Recipient web page or social media = documented **self-report** of activity, not approved City project scope.
- Organizer account of delivery = reported activity; cannot substitute for original close-out, attendee records, proof of payment or evaluator scores.
- Funder-published grant record = separate reported funding source, **not a receipt or evidence that the same cost was claimed twice**.
- Published collaboration = possible project partner, **not proof of family links, shared ownership, financial transfers or conflicts**.
- Repeated funding and annual renewal are explicitly allowed by the [City's grant eligibility presentation](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2025%20All%20Streams%20Eligibility%20and%20Timelines%20Presentation.pdf).

No further municipalities or other grant programs are added to the research scope: the Peel records serve only as contextual co-funder evidence for an existing Brampton grantee.
