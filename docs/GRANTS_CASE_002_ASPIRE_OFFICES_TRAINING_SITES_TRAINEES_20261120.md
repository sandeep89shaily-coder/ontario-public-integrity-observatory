# Case 002 addendum — Aspire office versus training sites and named public testimonials

**Research date: November 20, 2026. Public documentary investigation only; no external outreach authorized.**

- Aspire's published 2023 impact report gives its office as **Brampton, Ontario**, without a street address: https://aspireforhigher.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2023-A4H-Impact-Report-Updated-V4.pdf
- The **federal Lobbyists Registry** (historical 2024 public declaration) identifies Aspire for Higher parent company at **55 Ridgehill Drive, Brampton, L6Y 2C3**, and its Black Health Care Professional Network client at **45 Bramalea Rd, Suite 208-#143, Brampton, L6T 2W4**. These are **historic legal/administrative correspondence locations, not confirmed public-facing offices or training grounds**: https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/vwRg?blnk=1&cno=376996&regId=947285
- **Correction to potential misleading web results:** 60 West Drive, Brampton is listed on Aspire's resources page under **Peel Catholic Family Services**, a separate organization, not Aspire: https://aspireforhigher.ca/resources/
- A4H's public organizational contact is 647-799-3655; A4H Skills Lab: https://a4hskillslab.ca/contact/

## Actual training-provider pathway and campus verification status

A4H Skills Lab states that the 12-week EJEFT program offers subsidized electrical, plumbing or intro-home-renovation training **led by Skilled Trades College of Canada**. Course structure includes hands-on labs and collectively constructing a model/demo home: https://a4hskillslab.ca/programs/extra-judicial-employment-fast-track-program/

The A4H form publicly offers registration-area choices **Markham, Cambridge, Scarborough and Ajax**. This form does not establish campus attendance, 2024–25 cohort locations or exact funding periods.

## Publicly named participant (avoid profiling private beneficiaries)

**Baseer Ahmed** voluntarily spoke with **The Trillium** in an October 31, 2025 public interview about training through Aspire for Higher and stated that the program helped him secure a full-time carpentry job. Reporter describes SDF minister's office awarding $4.5m to the organization. Source: https://www.thetrillium.ca/news/politics/most-early-skills-development-fund-recipients-that-missed-a-key-training-or-employment-target-got-grants-again-11428916

**A graduate called Baseer** features in a separate public organizational testimonial video, with comments about support for skilled-trades training and an apprenticeship pathway: https://vimeo.com/1140188732 . It is **not yet proven** that this first-name-only video and The Trillium's identified interview concern the same person; do not count twice.

Ontario Legislature Hansard, October 21 2025, publicly welcomed **Eldon, Baseet, Jovens, Junior and Shayok** as visitors *from Aspire for Higher*: https://www.ola.org/en/legislative-business/house-documents/parliament-44/session-1/2025-10-21/hansard . They are **visitors and potentially staff/volunteers or participants**; not automatically graduates. Do not convert these names into trainee identities, infer criminal-justice histories or contact them.

**No public cohort participant roster was found.** For accountability, independent college aggregate attendance certificates, graduation counts by cohort, the training-provider contract, anonymized employment confirmations, ministry-accepted KPI reports, and supplier invoices are more suitable than collecting private trainee names or addresses.

## Open evidence questions

1. Which specific college campus and program were authorized in each SDF-funded cohort, and what were the exact service dates?
2. Does the public $4.5m award cover college tuition, issued tools, trainee supports, wages or employer placement services, and what expense records demonstrate delivery?
3. Are the college-issued course completion and financial records reconcilable to A4H's stated 52 graduates/34 jobs in 2024?
4. Is there a publicly verified *current* Aspire operating office? The historical federal lobbying addresses are not sufficient to confirm walk-in facilities.
5. Can funders provide privacy-preserving verified cohort totals, completion and employment figures, rather than personally identifying program participants?
