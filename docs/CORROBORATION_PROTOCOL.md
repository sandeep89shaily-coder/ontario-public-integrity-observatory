## Brampton grants: award vs outcome
- 2025 official recipient list contains 159 grant-table entries summing to **$1,750,000**, consistent with the [City's 2025 announcement](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/News/Pages/News-Release.aspx/1483).
- 2026 recipient list contains 137 grant-table entries summing to **$1,583,225**. This is an approved-award-list sum, not confirmed disbursement.
- 2025 rules require project close-out evidence of delivery, project expenses and outputs. Recipients without an approved close-out report can be ineligible for future funding. Do **not** assume any recipient lacked an approved report; no individual close-out or payment records have been obtained.
- [2025 guidelines](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2025%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund%20Guidelines%20V1.0.pdf); [2026 guidelines](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/City-Hall/grants/Documents/2026%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund/2026%20Advance%20Brampton%20Fund%20Guidelines%20Web%20V.1pdf.pdf); [grant dashboard](https://performancedashboard.brampton.ca/culture-community-well-being/grant-funding-directed-to-arts-and-culture-programs/) is an arts subset, not a total of all grants.

## Acceptance gates
- Test against live official documents; all unavailable sources must be explicit failures, not zeroes.
- Verify published amounts and reporting policy; separate those from actual payments.
- Obtain approval/exception case records and independent performance/close-out information.
- Reconcile individual decisions to verified records; keep identified unknowns as open questions.
- No AI model may promote unknowns to misconduct findings; human editorial and legal review required before adverse publication.
